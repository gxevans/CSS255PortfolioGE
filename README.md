# CS 255 Portfolio – DriverPass System

## Project Summary

The DriverPass project was about designing a system for a company that helps people prepare for their driving tests. The client wanted an online system where students could take practice tests, schedule driving lessons, and keep track of their progress. The system also needed to allow employees and administrators to manage appointments, student information, and other important records.

## What I Did Well

I think I did a good job understanding what the client needed and turning those needs into a system design. I worked on requirements and UML diagrams to show how different parts of the system would work together. I also tried to make the design easy to understand so that developers could use it as a guide when building the actual system.

## What I Would Improve

If I could go back and revise one part of my work, I would spend more time reviewing my UML diagrams and making sure every detail was clear. I would check that the relationships between different parts of the system made sense and that the diagrams were easy for someone else to follow. This would help prevent confusion during development and make the design even better.

## Understanding the User's Needs

I looked at what DriverPass wanted to accomplish and what its customers and employees would need from the system. Students needed a way to schedule lessons and practice for their driving tests, while employees needed tools to manage appointments and student information. I used those needs to guide my requirements and system design. Considering the user's needs is important because the system is supposed to solve real problems. If the design does not meet those needs, the finished system might not be useful or easy to use.

## My Approach to Software Design

When designing software, I think it is important to understand the problem first before deciding how to solve it. I would start by gathering requirements, asking questions, and figuring out what the users expect from the system. Then I would use tools like UML diagrams to organize the information and show how the system should work. In the future, I would also review my designs with other team members and get feedback before moving forward. This would help catch mistakes early and make sure everyone understands the plan.
